02 / THE PRINCIPLE

The music steers.
The supply drives.

A line-level input is a small instruction, not a reservoir of speaker power. Voltage-gain stages enlarge its voltage swing; output devices draw energy from a DC supply to deliver load current. Different classes describe how those output devices conduct, switch, or use their supply headroom.

Shown here as simplified, representative circuits. Actual designs vary in biasing, feedback, power supply, filtering and protection. Vacuum-tube high voltage can be lethal: these are educational diagrams, not build guides.
